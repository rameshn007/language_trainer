# European Portuguese Grammar Cheat Sheet

This document compiles the essential grammar rules and irregular verb conjugations specifically for European Portuguese (as spoken in Portugal).

---

## Part 1: Core Grammar Rules

### 1. Pronouns & The Formality Spectrum
In Portugal, how you address someone dictates the verb conjugation and dictates respect.

| Pronoun | Meaning | Usage Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eu** | I | Always used. |
| **Tu** | You (informal) | Friends, family, children, colleagues of similar age. |
| **Você** | You (semi-formal) | **Caution:** Often considered blunt or slightly rude in Portugal. Better to use the person's name or title with the 3rd person verb. |
| **O senhor / A senhora** | You (formal) | Older people, strangers, formal service encounters. |
| **Ele / Ela** | He / She | 3rd person singular. |
| **Nós** | We | Standard for "we" (pt-PT rarely uses "a gente" formally). |
| **Eles / Elas** | They | 3rd person plural. |

### 2. The Golden Rule: Present Continuous
To say you are *currently* doing something, Portugal uses a specific structure that differs entirely from Brazilian Portuguese.

* **Rule:** `Estar` (conjugated) + `a` + `Infinitive Verb`
* **Example:** "I am eating" -> *Eu estou a comer*. (Never *estou comendo*).
* **Example:** "She is working" -> *Ela está a trabalhar*.

### 3. Regular Present Tense Verbs
Verbs fall into three categories based on their ending.

| Subject | -AR (Falar: to speak) | -ER (Comer: to eat) | -IR (Partir: to leave) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eu** | fal**o** | com**o** | part**o** |
| **Tu** | fal**as** | com**es** | part**es** |
| **Ele/Ela/Senhor** | fal**a** | com**e** | part**e** |
| **Nós** | fal**amos** | com**emos** | part**imos** |
| **Eles/Elas/Senhores** | fal**am** | com**em** | part**em** |

### 4. Pronoun Placement (The Hyphen Rule)
In Portugal, object pronouns (me, te, se, o, a, nos, etc.) attach strictly to the **end** of the verb with a hyphen by default (Enclisis). 

* **Default (After the verb):** *Chamo-me João.* (I call myself João.)
* **Default:** *Dá-me o livro.* (Give me the book.)

**The Exceptions (Proclisis):** The pronoun jumps *before* the verb if the sentence contains:
1.  **Negative words:** *Não me chamo João.*
2.  **Question words:** *Como te chamas?*
3.  **Certain adverbs/conjunctions:** *Já me disseste.*

### 5. Mandatory Preposition Contractions
Portuguese heavily merges prepositions with definite articles (*o, a, os, as*).

| Preposition | + o (the - masc) | + a (the - fem) | Meaning Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **De** (of/from) | **do** | **da** | *O livro do João* (João's book) |
| **Em** (in/on/at) | **no** | **na** | *Moro na cidade* (I live in the city) |
| **A** (to/at) | **ao** | **à** | *Vou ao mercado* (I go to the market) |
| **Por** (by/for) | **pelo** | **pela** | *Vou pela praia* (I go by the beach) |

### 6. Gender & Plural Basics
* **Gender:** Words ending in **-o** are usually masculine. Words ending in **-a**, **-ção**, or **-dade** are usually feminine. 
* **Plurals:** Add **-s** to words ending in a vowel. Words ending in **-m** change to **-ns** (e.g., *um homem* -> *uns homens*).

## Part 2: Essential Irregular Verbs (Present Tense)

### 1. The Core Conjugations
| Pronoun | Ser (To be) | Estar (To be) | Ter (To have) | Ir (To go) | Fazer (To do/make) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eu** | sou | estou | tenho | vou | faço |
| **Tu** | és | estás | tens | vais | fazes |
| **Ele / Ela / O Senhor** | é | está | tem | vai | faz |
| **Nós** | somos | estamos | temos | vamos | fazemos |
| **Eles / Elas / Os Senhores**| são | estão | têm | vão | fazem |

> **Pronunciation Tip:** Notice the circumflex on the plural **têm** (*they have*). It is pronounced differently than the singular **tem** (*he/she has*). **Têm** sounds like a nasal "tãym", whereas **tem** sounds like a nasal "tãing".

### 2. Ser vs. Estar (The "To Be" Divide)
* **Ser (Permanent / Identity):** Used for origin, profession, physical traits, and time. (*Sou de Portugal.*)
* **Estar (Temporary / State / Location):** Used for location, emotions, weather, and the present continuous. (*Estou cansado.* / *Estamos a comer.*)

### 3. Ter (To Have) & Its Hidden Uses
* **Age:** *Tenho 40 anos.* (I have 40 years = I am 40 years old.)
* **Obligation:** *Tenho de ir ao supermercado.* (I have to go... use *ter de*, not *ter que* in Portugal.)

### 4. Ir (To Go) & The Near Future Hack
* **Movement:** *Vou a casa.*
* **The Future Hack (Ir + Infinitive):** *Vou cozinhar.* (I am going to cook.)

### 5. Fazer (To Do / To Make)
* **Actions:** *Faço o jantar.* (I make dinner.)
* **Weather/Time:** *Faz sol* (It's sunny). *Faz dois anos que moro aqui* (It's been two years...).

## Part 3: Essential Irregular Verbs (Simple Past / Pretérito Perfeito)

Use this tense for completed actions in the past that have a clear beginning and end.

### 1. The Irregular Past Tense Grid
**Note:** In the simple past tense, the verbs **Ser** (to be) and **Ir** (to go) share the exact same conjugations. Context determines meaning.

| Pronoun | Ser / Ir (To be / To go) | Estar (To be - temp) | Ter (To have) | Fazer (To do/make) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eu** | fui | estive | tive | fiz |
| **Tu** | foste | estiveste | tiveste | fizeste |
| **Ele / Ela / O Senhor** | foi | esteve | teve | fez |
| **Nós** | fomos | estivemos | tivemos | fizemos |
| **Eles / Elas / Os Senhores**| foram | estiveram | tiveram | fizeram |

> **European Portuguese Pronunciation Tip:** The "e" at the end of the *Tu* conjugations (*fost**e***, *estivest**e***, *tivest**e***, *fizest**e***) is almost entirely swallowed or silent in Portugal. It sounds more like "fost", "shtivest", etc. Also, the "s" right before the "t" is pronounced like an English "sh".

### 2. Contextual Examples
* **Ser:** *O jantar foi excelente.* (The dinner was excellent.)
* **Ir:** *Ela foi a Lisboa ontem.* (She went to Lisbon yesterday.)
* **Estar:** *Onde é que estiveste?* (Where were you?)
* **Ter:** *Ontem o meu filho teve febre.* (Yesterday my son had a fever.)
* **Fazer:** *O que é que fizeste?* (What did you do?)
